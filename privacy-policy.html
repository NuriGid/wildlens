Privacy Policy

Wildlens · Last updated: October 21, 2026

Wildlens: Plant & Bug ID ("Wildlens", "the app", "we") helps you identify trees, plants and insects from photos. We built it to collect as little data as possible. This policy explains what happens to your information.

1. Photos you scan

When you tap the shutter, the photo is resized on your device and sent over an encrypted connection to our identification server, which forwards it to Google's Gemini API to generate the result. We do not store your photos on our servers, and we do not use them to identify you. Google processes the image under the Gemini API Additional Terms of Service; Google may retain request data for a limited period for abuse monitoring, as described in those terms. Photos are not used by us for advertising or model training.

Before your first scan, the app asks for your permission to share photos with this AI service. You can decline, in which case no photo is sent.

2. Camera access

The app uses your camera only while you are on the scanning screen and only to take the photo you request. You can revoke camera access at any time in iOS Settings.

3. Subscriptions and purchases

Payments are processed entirely by Apple. We never see your name, card or Apple ID details. We use RevenueCat to verify subscription status; RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user identifier and your purchase receipt. See RevenueCat's Privacy Policy.

4. Data stored on your device

The app stores a few settings locally on your device: your remaining free scans, your subscription status cache, and whether you agreed to the AI photo notice. This data never leaves your device and is deleted when you delete the app.

5. What we do not do

We do not require an account, collect your name, email, contacts or location, show third-party ads, sell your data, or track you across other companies' apps and websites.

6. Children

Wildlens is suitable for all ages but is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Your rights

Because we do not keep personal data on our servers, there is usually nothing for us to access or delete. If you have a question or request under GDPR, CCPA or similar laws, contact us and we will respond within 30 days.

8. Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above. Significant changes will be shown in the app.
